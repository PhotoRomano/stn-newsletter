🔥 The Dormition Fast Begins Today

Today, August 14, the Church enters the Dormition Fast — a two-week period of fasting and prayer that prepares us for the Dormition of the Most Holy Theotokos on August 28.

The fast calls us to abstain from meat, dairy, fish, wine, and oil on stricter days — though fish is permitted on most days. The exception is the Transfiguration (August 19), when fish and wine are blessed and may be eaten freely, even during the fast.

Fast days (no meat, dairy, fish, wine, oil): Wednesdays & Fridays
Moderate fast (no meat, dairy; fish/wine/oil permitted): Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun
The Transfiguration (Aug 19): Fish, wine & oil permitted — Great Feast

“Fasting is the mother of prayer, the root of repentance, and the beginning of all good.” — St. Basil the Great

✨ Transfiguration — Tuesday, August 19

This Tuesday we celebrate one of the Twelve Great Feasts of the Church — the Transfiguration of Our Lord, God, and Savior Jesus Christ (Преображење Господње). On Mount Tabor, Christ revealed His divine glory to the apostles Peter, James, and John before His Passion.
