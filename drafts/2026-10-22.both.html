This Week’s Feature

Circle of Serbian Sisters Slava — This Sunday, Oct 25

Please join us this Sunday, Oct 25, as the Circle of Serbian Sisters celebrates its Slava, St. Petka — whose feast day is October 27 — with Divine Liturgy at 10:00 AM, followed by a festive luncheon. All are welcome to come celebrate with us.

Slava’s Kum: Petko Aleksić and his family.

Questions? Email hello@stnicholasphilly.org.

🏠 About the Special Assembly

The board is looking to build or buy real estate using funds already allocated for this purpose. At the Special Assembly, we're asking the community to authorize the board to decide on a property purchase if a good deal presents itself — without needing to call a separate assembly each time.

This would let us move quickly when the right property comes on the market. We hope you'll join us on Sunday, Nov 1.

🏠 О Ванредној скупштини

Одбор разматра куповину или изградњу некретнине користећи средства која су већ издвојена у ту сврху. На Ванредној скупштини тражимо од заједнице да овласти одбор да одлучи о куповини некретнине ако се укаже добра прилика — без потребе да се за сваку прилику сазива посебна скупштина.

Ово би нам омогућило да брзо реагујемо када се на тржишту појави права некретнина. Надамо се да ћете нам се придружити у недељу, 1. новембра.

Serbian Language School — Passing the Language Down

No class this Sunday, Oct 25 — we pause for the Slava. Bring your kids anyway: come be part of it and learn the tradition and customs of a Slava firsthand.

Every Liturgy we sing, every Slava we celebrate, every song and story we tell — our language carries all of it. Our Serbian School keeps that thread unbroken for the next generation.

Classes are running now, and enrollment stays open — join whenever you’re ready.
