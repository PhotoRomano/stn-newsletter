On the Horizon

Sun, Oct 25 Serbian elections — not a local Philadelphia vote; nearest polling site is the Serbian Consulate General in New York, roughly 1:00 AM–2:00 PM (registration closed Oct 3)
Sun, Oct 25 Circle of Serbian Sisters Slava: St. Petka — Liturgy & luncheon

Diaspora polling hours are set to match Serbia's own schedule (7:00 AM–8:00 PM Central European Time), so the overnight-into-afternoon window falls in the early morning locally. There is no Philadelphia polling site — only four in the U.S. (Washington, Chicago, New York, Los Angeles). Nearest: Serbian Consulate General, 62 West 45th Street, 7th Floor, New York, NY 10036. Confirm your registration and exact hours directly with the consulate before making the trip. More on the diaspora voting schedule →
